## Retention Model

P3.D verification reports are derived on demand.

- No verification-owned store.
- No infinite streams.
- No copied runtime payloads.
- Source refs and hashes only.
- Retention class: `P3D_DERIVED_VERIFICATION`.
- Existing P2.5/P3.A/P3.B retention rules remain authoritative.
