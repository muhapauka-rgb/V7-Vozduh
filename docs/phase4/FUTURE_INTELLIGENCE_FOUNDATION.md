# V7 Phase 4 - Future Intelligence Foundation

## Purpose

Prepare for predictive degradation and adaptive routing without AI routing.

## Allowed Future Intelligence

May:

- forecast degradation;
- recommend quarantine;
- prioritize diagnostics;
- explain likely causes;
- score route survivability.

Must never:

- silently reroute users;
- bypass policy;
- disable kill switch;
- override route classes;
- become black-box routing.

## Deterministic Core Rule

Even with future intelligence:

- policy engine stays authoritative;
- route classes stay authoritative;
- kill switch stays mandatory;
- autoswitch remains bounded and explainable.
