# V7 Phase 6 Future Operator Platform Foundation

## Purpose

Phase 6 should leave room for future operator intelligence without implementing heavy AI.

## Future Capabilities

- advanced diagnostics;
- AI-assisted explanations;
- predictive degradation summaries;
- multi-region operations;
- regional service impact;
- guided maintenance workflows.

## Phase Boundary

Do not implement:

- black-box recommendations;
- AI routing;
- distributed operator platform;
- predictive automation;
- noisy recommendation cockpit.

Future assistance must remain explainable, bounded, and operator-first.
