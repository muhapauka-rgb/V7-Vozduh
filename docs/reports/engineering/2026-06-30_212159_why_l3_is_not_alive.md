## Question 1: Why Is `v7-users-autoswitch.service` Inactive?

`v7-users-autoswitch.service` is a `Type=oneshot` unit:

```text
ExecStart=/usr/local/bin/v7-users-autoswitch --apply
```

For a oneshot unit, `inactive` after execution is normal. The service is not a resident runtime loop.

Production state:

- `v7-users-autoswitch.service active=inactive`
- `v7-users-autoswitch.service enabled=static`

This is expected.

## Question 2: Why Is `v7-users-autoswitch.timer` Inactive?

`v7-users-autoswitch.timer` is the old broad apply timer:

```text
OnUnitActiveSec=20s
Unit=v7-users-autoswitch.service
```

Production state:

- `v7-users-autoswitch.timer active=inactive`
- `v7-users-autoswitch.timer enabled=enabled`
- last trigger was more than one month earlier
- no next trigger

Canonical reason:

- SYSTEM_MAP says autoswitch service/timer are inactive and approved manual mode.
- Canonical Reference says timer-only movement is rejected as a product model.
- OMP says daemon/timer enablement requires `ENGINEERING_AUTHORITY`.
- L3 capability explicitly rejects timer/cron/periodic movement as triggers.

Therefore this timer being inactive is intentional safety posture, not an L3 bug.
