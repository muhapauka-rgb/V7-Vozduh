The final document now states the permanent certification philosophy:

- certification targets earned capability, not numeric configuration;
- controlled production certification creates repeatable real production conditions instead of waiting for random incidents;
- certification users participate temporarily while the production chain remains ordinary and real;
- production must be restored after certification;
- only historical engineering evidence may remain.
