## 1. Purpose

This discovery answers one question:

```text
What fundamental engineering model already lies underneath V7?
```

The discovery does not create a new architecture, owner, memory, Runtime, Planner, truth source, proof system, or knowledge system.

It evaluates whether V7 is fundamentally:

- Proof-Centric;

- Evidence-Centric;
- Verification-Centric;
- Traceability-Centric;
- Consumer-Chain-Centric;

| Candidate Model | Short Definition | Initial Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Canonical-Knowledge-Centric | Canonical knowledge is the highest authority and future work consumes it. | Strong for durable truth, but current reality can invalidate or route review; canonical knowledge does not prove runtime execution by itself. |

No claim, action, knowledge, state, report, learning, or evolution step may be promoted, consumed, closed, or made current unless it is proven through owner-backed evidence, verification, traceability, consumer confirmation, and terminal-state closure.

## 9. Architecture Analysis

V7 is not purely Knowledge-Centric.

Knowledge is a durable foundation, but it is not enough to:

- prove current runtime reality;
- authorize production action;
- update maturity;
- close a mission;
- evolve canonical truth;
- admit Behaviour Reality;
- update learning.

V7 is also not purely Behaviour-Centric.

Behaviour is the target of autonomous evolution, but behaviour admission depends on:

- evidence;
- truth hierarchy;
- traceability;
- verification;
- consumer path;
- terminal state.

V7 is not Evidence-Centric in the raw-data sense.

Evidence is everywhere, but raw evidence can be rejected, stale, synthetic, incomplete, ownerless, or insufficient.

V7 is not a standalone Proof system either.

There is no separate Proof owner, Proof storage, Proof Runtime, or Proof system.

The real existing architecture is:

```text
Proof-gated owner/consumer chain architecture
```

or, more explicitly:

```text
Evidence-verified traceable consumer-closure architecture.
```

This is a description of existing mechanisms, not a new architecture.
